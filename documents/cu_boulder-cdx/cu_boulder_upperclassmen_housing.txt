Title: CU Boulder housing for upperclassmen
Source URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/cuboulder/comments/15i3461
Source type: Reddit thread
Collected: 2026-06-06
Topic tags: upperclassmen housing, Bear Creek lottery, dorm priority, off-campus leases

The original poster knew that many juniors and seniors at CU Boulder live off campus. They asked whether freshmen are given priority in housing assignments and how likely it is that a junior or senior housing application would be accepted if they wanted to stay in residence halls all four years.

One student said that unless the student becomes an RA or gets accepted into Bear Creek, living in campus housing as an upperclassman is very unlikely. The commenter said Bear Creek is chosen through a lottery system, so there is no guarantee of getting in.

The same commenter said Boulder has a housing shortage that affects both dorms and off-campus housing. They said first-year students are prioritized for university housing, but even some first-year students had to live off campus because there was not enough dorm capacity. The commenter added that the ideal time to sign a lease would have been during the previous year.

Another student said they loved dorm life as a freshman but wanted to move into an apartment with friends after a year of sharing a room. They described sophomore year in an apartment as rewarding because it offered more adult independence and less oversight from RAs. They advised that if staying in campus housing is important, becoming an RA is a practical route.

The practical takeaway is that upperclassmen should not count on residence halls. Bear Creek is possible but uncertain, RA roles can help, and students should begin off-campus planning early.
