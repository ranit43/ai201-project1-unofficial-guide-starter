Title: CU Boulder meal plan types
Source URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/cuboulder/comments/14vmt6x
Source type: Reddit thread
Collected: 2026-06-06
Topic tags: meal plans, residence halls, block meals, graduate housing

The original poster asked about the difference between Residence Hall Student Meal Plans and Block Meal Plans. They were confused about whether a residence hall was the same thing as graduate housing such as Athens North.

A student answered that only students living in residence halls can get the residence hall meal plan, which they described as the 15 or 19 meals per week plan. The same commenter said block meal plans are for non-freshmen or students living off campus and work as a set number of meals for the semester, with the smallest option around 50 meals.

The commenter clarified that student living buildings are residence halls, but graduate housing is dedicated to graduate students. They added that most residence halls are for freshmen.

The practical advice from the thread is that students should choose meal plan options based on housing status. Residence hall plans are tied to residence hall living, while block plans are the flexible option for students who are not in freshman-style residence halls or who live off campus.
