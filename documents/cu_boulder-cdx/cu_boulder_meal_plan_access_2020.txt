Title: CU Boulder meal plan access and Bear Creek
Source URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/cuboulder/comments/i4x806
Source type: Reddit thread
Collected: 2026-06-06
Topic tags: meal plans, Bear Creek, dining halls, campus cash

The original poster asked whether returning students were allowed to get meal plans. They had read an occupancy management email and felt confused because it did not clearly say returning students could not buy a meal plan.

One student said a town hall had stated that campus planned to start with only students living on campus, but might open meal plans to other students later if the rollout went well.

Another student asked whether that applied to Bear Creek residents. A reply said Bear Creek residents were only able to use campus cash at restaurants, cafes, and markets. According to that reply, Bear Creek residents would not be able to go to dining halls at Village Center, C4C, UMC, or Sewall under that policy.

This thread is useful because it shows a common unofficial-knowledge problem: students were trying to infer practical dining access from partial or unclear university communication. The main student takeaway was that Bear Creek and returning-student dining access may differ from regular residence hall access, and official communications should be checked closely.
