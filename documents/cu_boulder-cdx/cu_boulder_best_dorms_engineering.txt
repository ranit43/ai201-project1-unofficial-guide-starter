Title: CU Boulder dorms for engineering freshmen
Source URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/cuboulder/comments/1jjba3x
Source type: Reddit thread
Collected: 2026-06-06
Topic tags: dorm selection, engineering, bathrooms, AC, Will Vill, Kittredge, Stearns

The original poster asked which dorms are best for a first-year engineering student, especially if they want an attached bathroom instead of a communal bathroom and air conditioning.

One student said they lived in a single in Williams Village North with an attached bath during freshman year and found it very nice.

Another student recommended the Kittredge area. A reply said Kittredge felt chill because it had the pond, was slightly off to the side, avoided heavy crowds, and was still on campus. The same student contrasted that with Hallett, which was close to C4C and socially positive but had tiny rooms.

Students also discussed Reed as a good option for break housing. One comment called Reed the best dorm on campus for students who need break housing.

Another student said Stearns may be one of the options with apartment-style rooms, where a bathroom is shared by a small group rather than an entire hall. The thread suggests that students looking for private or semi-private bathrooms should pay close attention to individual dorm room layouts rather than assuming all residence halls are the same.

The practical takeaway is that Williams Village and Stearns may be stronger for attached or semi-private bathroom preferences, while Kittredge is often described as quieter and still reasonably connected to campus.
