Title: CU Boulder meal plan costs and dining hall price
Source URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/cuboulder/comments/hh8btj
Source type: Reddit thread
Collected: 2026-06-06
Topic tags: meal plans, dining costs, Bear Creek, block meals

A student discussing meal plans said that paying for a CU dining hall with a credit card was roughly double the cost of using meal swipes. They remembered that the previous year it was around $13 to enter the Williams Village dining center with a card, while a meal swipe on a larger block meal plan cost around $6.80.

The same commenter described confusion about Bear Creek residents and block meal plans. They said concern started after students saw a weirdly worded Q&A suggesting Bear Creek residents would no longer be able to use meal plans because of dining hall traffic restrictions. The commenter personally called the Bear Creek front desk and was told the person there had not heard about official changes to Bear Creek block meal swipes.

The commenter interpreted the situation as another example of poor coordination and poor communication from CU Boulder. They argued that if Bear Creek students were not allowed to buy block meal plans, CU needed to communicate that clearly before students moved back.

The practical student knowledge in this thread is that block meal swipes can be much cheaper than paying dining hall door prices, but meal plan eligibility and Bear Creek access may not always be clearly communicated.
